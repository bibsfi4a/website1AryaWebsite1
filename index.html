Competition

In the next few months, we will launch a competition to find the best local development project. We are looking for any policy, initiative, technology, or business at any stage of completion. The goal is to see the real-world impact of our education. We want to empower people to use their knowledge to improve their own communities. We will work directly with our local partner NGOs and they will serve as the main point of contact for all local projects.
