Our Team

Jonas Loening

Development First Co-Founder

Jonas is a co-founder of Development First. Born in the United States and educated in both Europe and America, he brings an international perspective to community impact and youth engagement.

Passionate about finance, technology, and global development, Jonas is committed to helping students turn ideas into action through innovative projects and cross-cultural collaboration. Through Development First, he works to connect young leaders across different countries and provide them with the tools, mentorship, and opportunities needed to address local challenges and contribute to a more sustainable future.

Ibrahim Garba

Founder & CEO, AYSA Africa

Ibrahim Garba is a development strategist and social entrepreneur committed to advancing inclusive and sustainable transformation across Africa. He is the Founder and CEO of AYSA Africa and serves as President of FIJADA Cameroon.

His work focuses on youth empowerment, policy engagement, and strategic governance, promoting participatory and locally driven development aligned with global priorities. Through AYSA Africa, he leads initiatives that strengthen leadership, foster innovation, and support community-led solutions.

At FIJADA Cameroon, he contributes to national and international dialogues on development, migration, and economic growth, championing the role of African youth as key drivers of transformative change.

Alphon Percival Dalanguere

CYPA Founder

Alphon Percival Dalanguere is an African impact entrepreneur and sustainable development strategist dedicated to empowering youth and women. As the founder of the Central African Young Professionals Association (CYPA), One4greenafrica, and Gozo Resilience Hub, he leads initiatives focused on climate action, entrepreneurship, and professional training in the Central African Republic.

His global leadership includes prestigious programs like the YALI Mandela Washington Fellowship and roles with the United Nations Volunteers. An MBA graduate and international interpreter, Alphon combines academic expertise with field experience to build resilient economic ecosystems across Africa.
